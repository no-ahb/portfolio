About New York, NY

Noah Berrie

Technical builder, artist, and educator.

studio@noahberrie.com

I build software, instruments, and installations. My work connects interaction design with the code, electronics, and physical construction that make something work. I work directly with the people using what I build, whether that means a tutoring platform or a room-scale sound installation.

I studied Music, Science & Technology at Stanford's CCRMA, following degrees in Symbolic Systems and Music. Alongside my own projects, I teach, work with artists, and score for film and video.
