02 / What is currently competing

The current site has an appealing premise: a flat field lets the drawings, photographs, and typography belong to one page. The difficulty comes from several independent color systems acting on that field.

  1. The foreground has several owners. The background script switches ink at a luminance threshold of 0.20. The type study fixes title letters at #262626 and turns them rust on hover. Storm & silver adds a broad black override; side labels still use brown. A visitor can see cream body copy and dark title letters on the same blue.
  2. The switch happens before the background finishes. Text changes immediately when a section crosses 45% of the viewport; the ground takes 650 ms. During a dark-to-light change, dark ink can appear while the old dark ground is still visible.
  3. Headings determine the palette rhythm. The section index is reduced modulo the palette length. Adding a subsection can change every later color; tightly spaced headings can trigger a quick sequence of full-page changes. The order has little connection to reading density or project identity.
  4. Image treatment adds another moving color relationship. Every main-content image is grayscaled and multiplied; a separate overlay restores some color on hover. That treatment also reaches evidence such as screenshots and spectrograms. It can conceal information and makes the same field feel much denser around photographs.
Measured example: Open water's #427D99 gives 4.18:1 with the current cream ink, below the 4.5:1 ordinary-text minimum. On #454B7E, fixed #262626 title letters give 1.85:1, below even the 3:1 large-text threshold. Storm's black text already measures 7.87:1 or better at its anchors; the request for more richness is a separate design issue.

Source: the local portfolio CSS and runtime, inspected 10 October 2026. The Open water foreground mismatch was also verified in the browser. These examples are color-pair measurements, not a full accessibility audit.

The pale and strong versions are roles within one family. Use a strong ground for an opening or a spacious drawing; move toward its pale relative for sustained reading. Avoid assigning a new hue to each subsection. The associations above are editorial choices for this work, not universal meanings of color.

These colors are proposed screen colors, not samples of Turner paintings. Their lightness and chroma vary by hue: yellow can remain intensely yellow at high luminance, while blue needs a lighter value to support black. Equal HSL saturation or equal OKLCH lightness would not guarantee equal legibility.

The numerical check and its limits

The study sampled all 100 ordered pairs of these ten grounds at 1,001 Oklab interpolation steps each (100,100 samples). The lowest black contrast was 7.44:1. Maximum sRGB gamut excursion was 0.000000. The calculations use WCAG relative luminance after conversion to sRGB. This validates the color paths, not typography, images, overlays, anti-aliasing, or every control in the portfolio. The specimen's displayed ratio uses the same interpolated channels as its CSS.

04 / Where the richness comes from

Give strong color an unequal share of attention. A saturated blue after paper feels more pronounced than the same blue inside an uninterrupted sequence of equally strong colors. For a first composition, give roughly two-thirds of the reading journey to paper, silver, and pale fields, and one-third to stronger passages. This is a starting art-direction choice, not a color-theory formula.

Keep the material associations broad. North Sea supports blue and silver. ANTIPHON contains copper, wood, brass, paper, and recorded sound; its warm family can be red with a restrained yellow interval. Suspension benefits from silver, pale warmth, and black wire drawings. Green can give the performance software its own quiet register. Shared paper and black ink connect these projects without forcing every work into a seascape.

Use size and space to establish hierarchy. Keep captions, links, side labels, and diagram labels black. Smaller size and different spacing are enough to distinguish them. Use black outlines, underlines, dash patterns, and explicit labels for states. Remove unnecessary rules; retain the useful ones in black instead of making an entire network of faint gray lines.

Protect color that is evidence. Keep screenshots, spectrograms, and data maps faithful. If photographic multiply stays, opt individual atmospheric images into it and give readers an explicit original-color control. A technical plot should never depend on hover to become intelligible. Colored data marks may remain when meaningful, with labels or shapes; their text and structural lines remain black.

The limit of permanent black

Deep cobalt #264ABF gives only 2.81:1 with black. Preserve that exact depth in a text-free field, or place the reading on paper beside it. A whole-page deep blue with black body text cannot satisfy this brief.

05 / Compose a slower scroll

Use one flat ground sampled continuously from scroll position. Author stable stops for actual project sections. Let a ground hold through the central reading area, then change over roughly one viewport in the space between passages. Long text sections can keep their color for several viewports. The precise distances should follow the content.

Within a family, move between pale and strong versions. Between unrelated strong hues, travel through a chosen pale or neutral stop. Blue directly into yellow can create an unwanted intermediate color; a deliberate silver interval makes that passage intelligible. Oklab gives useful perceptual interpolation, but the route still needs composition.

The prototype has one short 120 ms settling filter for abrupt input, no second CSS background transition, and no movement while idle after settling. Its miniature sequence demonstrates holds and quiet bridges. Production timing should be attached to the real sections rather than this study's normalized scroll distance. Keep the rendered ground continuous across route changes. Reduced motion and the study's pause control hold paper.

06 / What to take from theory and current practice

Albers: evaluate color in context. A swatch's effect changes with its neighbors and area. The relevant comparison is this site's actual text, image, and line density against a ground. The specimen above provides that comparison.

Turner's Sea View: retain the full chromatic range. The museum describes warm clouds, blue and violet, gold sails, and green water. A sea reference need not produce an all-sage or all-slate palette. My application here is to keep the varied hue families while controlling the areas and intervals in which they appear.

Skowhegan: a useful existing reference. The repository's earlier reference study documents flat category grounds, black text, and selectively restored photographic color. The useful principle for this portfolio is a consistent foreground on expressive fields. Its exact colors and photograph treatment need not become a template for technical evidence.

CSS Color 4 and Björn Ottosson's Oklab work provide practical tools for controlling perceived lightness and interpolating colors. Author the families in OKLCH; interpolate the chosen paths in Oklab. Keep a verified sRGB baseline. Neither space guarantees contrast, harmony, or good gamut behavior automatically.

WCAG 2.2 sets 4.5:1 for ordinary text and 3:1 for large text. Use 7:1 as this design's more generous target for reading. Meaningful controls and graphics need 3:1 where that criterion applies. Thin lines need visual inspection even when their nominal colors pass.

Contemporary color tools help make a bold design predictable. They do not supply a new universal law of beautiful palettes. The choice to keep black ink, unequal color durations, and a limited set of recurring families is the art direction proposed here.

07 / Simplify the implementation

  1. Give one theme ownership of ink, rules, ground, and the neutral surface. Remove the light-ink branch and reconcile fixed heading, hover, sidebar, SVG, and canvas colors.
  2. Replace the eight competing palette presets with these named families and explicit section assignments. Retire the public palette picker once a direction is chosen.
  3. Replace the modulo section switch and 650 ms CSS fade with one continuous scroll sampler. Keep a static paper fallback, reduced-motion behavior, and route continuity.
  4. Make image blending opt-in. Audit active, focused, disabled, audio, and diagram states on the strongest ground and through transitions.

This page is a local proposal in the existing color-study area. It does not apply a new theme to the portfolio. The full-page study uses representative project material so the direction can be judged before a site-wide change.
